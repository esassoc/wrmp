Layers

    Benchmark sites

    Historic, minimally modified mature tidal wetlands that represent pre-colonial ecosystems.

    Reference sites

    Century-old fringing wetlands, well-established restoration projects, and other mature marshes that represent reasonable habitat development endpoint projections for newer restoration projects.

    Project sites

    Recently restored tidal wetlands that represent a variety of design and management approaches.
